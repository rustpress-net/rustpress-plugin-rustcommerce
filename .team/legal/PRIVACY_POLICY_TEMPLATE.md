### 5.2 How We Protect Your Data

We implement the following security measures to protect your personal information:

- **Encryption in Transit**: All data transmitted between your browser and our servers is encrypted using TLS 1.2 or higher (HTTPS)
- **Encryption at Rest**: Customer personal data is encrypted in our database [specify encryption method if applicable]
- **Access Controls**: Access to customer data is restricted to authorized personnel on a need-to-know basis through role-based access control
- **Payment Security**: Credit card data is never stored on our servers; all payment processing is handled by Stripe (a PCI-DSS Level 1 certified provider)
- **Secure Authentication**: Passwords are hashed using industry-standard algorithms and are never stored in plain text
- **Webhook Verification**: All payment callbacks are cryptographically verified to prevent tampering
- **Audit Logging**: Access to sensitive data and financial transactions is logged for security monitoring
- **Regular Updates**: Our platform and its dependencies are regularly updated to address security vulnerabilities

### 5.3 Data Breach Response

In the event of a data breach that poses a risk to your rights and freedoms, we will:

- Notify the relevant supervisory authority within 72 hours (where required by GDPR)
- Notify affected individuals without undue delay if the breach poses a high risk
- Document the breach, its effects, and remedial actions taken
